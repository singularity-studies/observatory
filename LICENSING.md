# Licensing

No project-wide license has been selected.

Until a license is adopted, the presence of material in this public repository does not grant reuse rights beyond those supplied by applicable law or GitHub's terms. Do not assume that source evidence can be redistributed merely because its metadata is recorded here.

Before Wave 0, governance must decide and document:

- a license for software;
- a license for original documentation;
- a license or terms for released datasets;
- handling of third-party source material and metadata; and
- attribution and citation requirements.

Source records should prefer locators, metadata, and lawful excerpts over copied third-party content. Any adopted license must be reflected consistently in repository files and release metadata.
