## Education

- Outcome: `not_coextensive_for_normalization`
- Criteria: `same_substantive_locus = true`;
  `equivalent_inclusion_envelope = false`;
  `equivalent_exclusion_envelope = false`;
  `no_material_scope_asymmetry = false`; `lens_difference_only = false`
- Decisive scope difference: FORD 5.3 classifies R&D subject-matter content in
  Education and excludes routine education or training that is not R&D. ISIC
  85 classifies education provision, instruction, administration,
  intermediation, and support while placing relevant R&D outside the Division.
  The economic-activity envelope is materially different from the R&D-content
  envelope.
- Official clarification: OECD Frascati Manual 2015, Chapter 2.6, Table 2.2,
  and relevant Chapters 2 and 9 distinctions between R&D and education or
  training; UN ISIC Revision 5 explanatory notes, Section Q, Division 85 and
  descendants 851 through 856.
